## Chapter 12: Full AZ-104 Exam Simulation and Test-Taking Strategy — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | AZ-104 exam time budget: how many minutes are allocated for the exam content itself? | 100 minutes for exam content (total appointment time is longer due to sign-in, NDA, and survey). Exam tip: mentally checkpoint your pace at the 25%, 50%, and 75% question marks against elapsed time. |
| 2 | AZ-104 passing score threshold? | 700 out of 1000 (standard Microsoft certification passing score scale). Exam tip: this is a scaled score, not a raw percentage — don't try to calculate a percentage from it. |
| 3 | If a case study block contains 6 questions and takes 12 minutes to read and answer, how should you treat the remaining time budget? | Subtract both the time spent AND the questions answered from your totals before recalculating minutes-per-remaining-question. Exam tip: recompute pace after any block that took non-uniform time, don't assume the original average still applies. |
| 4 | Typical size of a case study question block on a Microsoft role-based exam if present? | Commonly 5-7 linked questions per case study scenario. Exam tip: budget extra upfront reading time once per case study, not per question within it. |
| 5 | Case study strategy pattern: read the scenario how many times before answering its questions? | Once, thoroughly — noting constraints like budget, region, compliance, and existing infrastructure. Exam tip: re-reading the full scenario per question wastes time you don't have. |
| 6 | Elimination technique pattern for multiple choice under time pressure? | 1) Discard options clearly unrelated to the tech/requirement. 2) Apply domain cues (cost, effort, availability) to remaining options. 3) Flag if still unsure, move on. Exam tip: eliminating even one wrong option meaningfully improves guess odds. |
| 7 | Flag-and-review workflow: correct order of operations? | 1) Answer every question with a best guess (never leave blank). 2) Flag uncertain ones as you go. 3) Finish the full exam first. 4) Return only to flagged questions with remaining time. Exam tip: never stop mid-exam to perfect one question. |
| 8 | Domain cue: 'least administrative effort' points to which type of solution? | Managed/PaaS/automated solutions over manual or IaaS-heavy ones, even if slightly costlier. Exam tip: this phrase is a deliberate signal, not filler wording. |
| 9 | Domain cue: 'highest availability' or 'minimize downtime' typically points to which architecture choice? | Solutions with redundancy across zones/regions (Availability Zones, geo-redundant storage, load balancing) over single-instance or single-region designs. Exam tip: match the cue word to the specific redundancy feature being tested. |
| 10 | Mixed question format time rule: drag-and-drop / hot-area / build-list questions vs. standard multiple choice? | Budget slightly more time due to interaction overhead, but still cap effort and flag if uncertain rather than perfecting it. Exam tip: attempt a best-guess ordering/placement rather than leaving it blank — partial reasoning still has a chance of being correct. |
| 11 | Anxiety/second-guessing rule for changing a flagged answer near the end of the exam? | Only change an answer if you can articulate a specific, concrete reason (new insight or found error). Never change based purely on stress or 'gut feeling' with no new information. Exam tip: repeated back-and-forth flipping wastes time and rarely improves accuracy. |
| 12 | Navigating the review screen: what should you check before final submission? | Confirm zero unanswered questions remain and that all flagged items have been revisited at least once. Exam tip: an unanswered question always scores zero — a guess always has a nonzero chance of being correct. |
