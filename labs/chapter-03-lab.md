# Chapter 3 Lab: Governance: Management Groups, Azure Policy, Locks, and Cost Controls

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Create an Azure Policy definition that denies resource creation outside two allowed regions, assign it at subscription scope, then create a budget in Cost Management with an alert at 80% of the monthly threshold.

## Key takeaway

Governance tools work together but solve different problems — management groups organize scope, Azure Policy enforces configuration, locks prevent accidental changes, and budgets control spend.
